Relay test, before you solder the relay driver (CW-020: pins IN · VCC · GND, no jumper)

Modules with these labels come in two kinds, so test yours. It takes 30 seconds and decides whether Q1 is fitted.

  1. Module alone: VCC to +5 V, GND to GND (the bank through J1, or the ESP32's 5V and GND pins). Its power LED lights; the relay must not click yet. No light: no 5 V, which is also why your relay never clicked before.
  2. Touch a wire from IN to GND. Click and relay LED on → low-level input → option A: fit R5 1 kΩ, R6 10 kΩ and Q1 BC547B exactly as drawn.
  3. No click: touch IN to 3V3 (J1-1). Click → high-level input → option B: no R5, R6 or Q1; wire GPIO10 (J1-16) straight to IN.
  4. Neither clicks with 5 V on VCC: the module is faulty; replace it.
  5. Screw terminal, with a meter on continuity: relay off → COM–NC closed, COM–NO open. Wire COM to INA219 VIN− and fan + (red) to NO.

Same firmware for both options (GPIO10 HIGH = fan ON), so the board needs no reflash.

PB1 is rated 12 000 mAh at 5 V 3 A (about 60 Wh delivered of its 74 Wh). At a typical 7–8 W the system runs about 7–8 hours; with everything at peak, about 4.5 hours. The Pi figures are Raspberry Pi's own (3 A supply recommended; 600 mA typical bare board); keep USB drives and screens off the Pi.

Before the first power-up

  1. U1 alone: connect VIN to the bank through J1 and F1, meter on VOUT+/VOUT−, turn the screw until it reads 12.0 V. Unplug.
  2. Relay test done and option A or B chosen (section 3). Fan + on relay NO, not NC.
  3. Check polarity: C1, C2 (stripe = −), D2, D3 (band = cathode), Q1 if fitted (flat face, 1 C · 2 B · 3 E).
  4. Continuity: J1 GND to ESP32 GND, U1 VIN−/VOUT−, Q1 emitter and every module GND.
  5. No continuity between +12 V, +5 V and +3V3, or from any rail to GND. J1 D− / D+ go nowhere.
  6. OLED, BME280, BH1750, INA219 VCC on 3V3; PIR, relay, MQ-135 VCC and U1 VIN+ on 5V.
  7. First power-up without the ESP32 in its headers: plug J1 into PB1 USB 1 and measure ≈ 5.0 V on the rail and 12.0 V on C2. Then insert the ESP32.
  8. Final order: both cables into PB1 (USB 1 → J1, USB 2 → Pi), then press PB1's button once. On the Pi, vcgencmd get_throttled must answer 0x0 (no under-voltage).

Expected readings

StateBus VCurrentPower
Relay off≈ 12.0 V0 mA0 W
Relay on, fan running≈ 11.9 V80–200 mA1–2.4 W

+5 V rail under load: 4.8–5.1 V; ESP32 5V pin about 0.3 V lower (D3). GPIO1 in clean air: about 0.2–0.5 V. Bus voltage near 0 V: U1 not set, INA219 GND or VIN− not wired. Negative current: VIN+ and VIN− swapped. Fan never stops: fan + on NC instead of NO, or the wrong relay option (repeat the relay test). Relay never clicks: no 5 V on its VCC pin, or Q1 legs reversed. Pi reboots when the fan starts: total load over 3 A or a thin Pi cable; use a short 3 A cable.
