If your HW-478 light module already has resistors on its board (small parts marked 151, 221 or similar), skip R3–R5 for that module only. The I²C modules already carry pull-up resistors, so no extra pull-ups are needed for four devices on short wires.

The 12 V fan branch, in current order

  1. J1 +12 V → F1 1 A → D1 1N5822 (band towards the circuit).
  2. After D1: C1 to ground, one wire to LM2596 IN+, one to INA219 VIN+.
  3. INA219 VIN− → relay COM; relay NO → fan +. NC stays empty.
  4. Fan − → ground. D2 1N4007 across the fan, band on fan +.
  5. INA219 GND to the same ground, or it cannot read the bus voltage.

Before you solder and power up

  • Set the LM2596 to 5.0–5.1 V with a multimeter before anything else is connected.
  • Check polarity of C1, C2 (stripe = −) and D1, D2 (band = cathode).
  • Continuity: J1 − to ESP32 GND to every module GND.
  • No continuity between 12 V and 5 V, or 5 V and 3.3 V.
  • OLED, BME280, BH1750 and INA219 VCC go to 3.3 V; PIR, relay and MQ-135 to 5 V.
  • Put the ESP32 on female headers so it can be swapped or flashed off the board.
  • First power-up without the ESP32: measure 12 V, 5 V. Then plug it in.
